# Booking and cancellation

## Making a booking
Tell the assistant your pickup area, the patient's name, a phone number, the hospital and the ambulance type. The assistant shows a summary and books only after you confirm.

## Emergencies
In an emergency, tell the assistant the pickup area and the nearest free ambulance is sent immediately. Always call 112 as well.

## Cancelling
You can cancel free of charge before the ambulance has started moving. After that, a cancellation fee of Rs 200 applies. Cancellation requests are handled by the dispatcher.

## Documents
You do not need any documents to book. A referral letter, if you have one, can be uploaded in the chat to fill in the details.
