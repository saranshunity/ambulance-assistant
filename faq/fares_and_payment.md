# Fares and payment

## How the fare is calculated
The fare is a base amount plus a rate per kilometre. Basic: Rs 500 plus Rs 15 per km. Oxygen: Rs 800 plus Rs 20 per km. ICU: Rs 2000 plus Rs 40 per km. Use the fare estimate in the chat for the exact amount.

## Payment
Payment is made to the ambulance crew at the end of the trip, in cash or UPI. You do not pay online while booking.

## Waiting charges
Waiting up to 10 minutes at pickup is free. After that, Rs 100 per 10 minutes is added.
