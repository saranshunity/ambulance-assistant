# Ambulance types

## Basic
A basic ambulance is for transport without special medical equipment. It has a stretcher and a trained driver.

## Oxygen
An oxygen ambulance has everything in a basic ambulance plus an oxygen supply for patients who have been prescribed oxygen.

## ICU
An ICU ambulance carries equipment for continuous patient monitoring and a medical attendant. It costs more than the other types.

## Choosing a type
The dispatcher can help you choose. If a referral letter or a doctor specifies a type, choose that one.
